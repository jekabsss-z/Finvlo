Questions

Frequently asked.

Is this investment advice?

No. Finvlo gives you an objective quality score on a business — data for your own research. We never tell you what to buy or sell, and we don’t score price.

Do I need to be good with numbers?

No. You send the company (attach financials or let us find them), and you get a clear score with a plain-language breakdown. Our guides explain every part.

Where do the sample scores come from?

The examples use illustrative sample data to show how a score looks. They’re demonstrations, not recommendations.

Who is behind Finvlo?

Two students building it in the open from Riga. Follow along on the blog.
